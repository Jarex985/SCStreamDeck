Shows the elapsed time in minutes and tenths of a minute and the estimated distance (meters, then kilometers from 1 km) of the measurement started with the Travel Timer key. The values freeze when the measurement is stopped. The distance is estimated from the ship's speed, so it follows the path flown. The game's telemetry server must be enabled.
